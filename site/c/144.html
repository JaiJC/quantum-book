Superconducting qubits and circuit QED

In 1985 John Clarke, Michel Devoret and John Martinis cooled an electrical circuit, a superconducting loop interrupted by a thin insulating barrier, and showed that its current tunnelled through an energy barrier and occupied discrete energy levels, like a single atom. A circuit made of billions of electrons behaved as one quantum object. They received the 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics. Four decades on, that discovery is the basis of the quantum chips built by Google, IBM and many others.

On the plate: design a superconducting qubit by choosing two energies.

From LC circuit to transmon

An inductor and capacitor form a harmonic oscillator; quantized, it has evenly spaced levels (Chapter 35), so you cannot address just two of them. A Josephson junction, two superconductors separated by a nanometre of oxide, acts as a nonlinear inductor with energy −EJcos⁡φ-E_J\cos\varphi−EJ​cosφ. The circuit Hamiltonian is

Circuit QED and readout

Couple the transmon to a microwave resonator. The Jaynes–Cummings model, built on the quantized field of Chapter 66, describes the exchange of excitations with coupling ggg. When the qubit and resonator are detuned by Δ≫g\Delta\gg gΔ≫g, they no longer exchange energy, but the resonator’s frequency shifts by ±χ≈±g2/Δ\pm\chi\approx\pm g^2/\Delta±χ≈±g2/Δ depending on the qubit state. Send a microwave tone and the reflected signal reveals the qubit without exciting it: dispersive readout. Coupling g/2π=g/2\pi =g/2π= 100 MHz, detuning 1.5 GHz. Two-qubit gates use tunable couplers to bring states into resonance briefly (Google’s CZ), or drive one qubit at the other’s frequency (IBM’s cross-resonance). Alexandre Blais and colleagues reviewed the field in 2021. Established

Why so cold, and where qubits lose energy

A 5 GHz photon carries energy hfhfhf equal to kB×0.24k_B\times0.24kB​×0.24 K. At 20 mK, the thermal excited population is e−12≈6×10−6e^{-12}\approx6\times10^{-6}e−12≈6×10−6; at 4 K a resonator holds 16 thermal photons. Hence dilution refrigerators (Chapter 149). Coherence is limited mainly by microscopic two-level-system defects in amorphous oxides at surfaces and interfaces, which absorb energy at random frequencies, and by stray quasiparticles. Materials science drives progress: in November 2025 a Princeton group reported transmons built from tantalum on high-resistivity silicon with lifetimes above 1 millisecond, an average quality factor of 9.7 million across 45 qubits. Google’s Willow has 105 qubits; IBM’s roadmap centres on qLDPC codes with long-range couplers (Chapter 141). Established

What does large E_J/E_C buy?tap
Exponential insensitivity to offset charge, at a modest cost in anharmonicity.
How does dispersive readout avoid exciting the qubit?tap
