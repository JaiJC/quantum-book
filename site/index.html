Who this is for

Readers who already have first-year university maths and want to learn quantum mechanics properly, all the way to quantum field theory, quantum computing and quantum biology. It was written with engineers, chemists and life scientists with a quantitative background in mind, and suits physics students who want a second pass.

What you need first

Comfort with derivatives and integrals of one variable, vectors and matrices, and school-level trigonometry and complex numbers. Part 0 (Chapters 2–21) is a fast refresher of the maths and classical physics the rest relies on. It is not a first course: if calculus or linear algebra is new to you, learn those first, then come back.

How to read it

Read the parts in order. Every chapter asks you to Predict first: answer before you scroll on, then test your answer on the simulation. The Aa button sets the depth: Intuition keeps the story and hides most equations. Every claim is labelled Established, Debated or Speculative.
