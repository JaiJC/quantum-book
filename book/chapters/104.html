A real qubit never evolves by a clean unitary. It leaks energy to its surroundings, its phase wanders, stray fields jostle it. Every such process, however messy, fits one mathematical form: a quantum channel. For a single qubit, any channel can be pictured as squashing, shifting and rotating the Bloch ball into an ellipsoid inside it. And every channel is fully specified by a single 4 × 4 matrix.

On the plate: the Bloch ball deformed by a noisy channel.

What a channel must be

A channel {{tex:\mathcal E}} maps density matrices to density matrices. It must be linear, preserve the trace, and keep states positive, even when it acts on part of a larger entangled system. That last condition, complete positivity, is stronger than positivity. The transpose map is positive, but applied to one half of a Bell pair it produces a matrix with eigenvalue −½ (Chapter 50): not a valid state. So transposition is not a physical channel. Physical processes are completely positive and trace-preserving: CPTP. Established

Three representations

Kraus: every CPTP map can be written

{{texd:\mathcal E(\rho) = \sum_kK_k\,\rho\,K_k^\dagger, \qquad \sum_kK_k^\dagger K_k = \mathbb 1}}

Stinespring: equivalently, attach an environment in a fixed state, apply a joint unitary, and discard the environment. Every channel is a unitary on a bigger system with part thrown away; noise is entanglement with what you don’t see (Chapter 52). Choi–Jamiołkowski: apply the channel to one half of a maximally entangled pair; the resulting 4 × 4 matrix, the Choi matrix, determines the channel completely, and the channel is CP exactly when that matrix is positive.

Three questions to carry with you.

Why isn’t the transpose a physical channel?tap
Applied to half of an entangled pair it gives a negative eigenvalue: it is positive but not completely positive.
What does amplitude damping do to the Bloch ball?tap
Shrinks it and shifts it toward |0⟩.
Why is T₂ ≤ 2T₁?tap
Energy relaxation alone already destroys coherence at rate 1/(2T₁); extra dephasing only adds to it.
